# The engravings

Each picture in the game is an engraving from the 1873 edition of *Around the
World in Eighty Days*, in the Towle translation, published at Boston by J. R.
Osgood & Co., after the drawings by Alphonse de Neuville and Léon Benett. They
were taken from the Internet Archive's scan of the New York Public Library's
copy, item `aroundworldineig00vern`, which the Archive records as not in
copyright (`possible-copyright-status: NOT_IN_COPYRIGHT`). Wikimedia Commons
holds the same engravings, each marked Public domain, and its descriptions
supplied the captions below, checked against each page as fetched.

Each was fetched on 2026-09-27 from
`https://archive.org/download/aroundworldineig00vern/page/n<index><scale>.jpg`,
cropped to the engraving, leaving out the page's margins and printed caption,
and saved as a JPEG within the plan's budget of 1600 pixels wide and about
300 KB each. The scan's own index is given, so any one can be fetched again.

**Two notes on the fetch.** The Commons descriptions place each engraving by
scan page counted from one, which is the Archive's index plus one for all
but one of these. The exception is the attack near Fort Kearney, which Commons
places four leaves late; the Archive's own leaf record put it at index 350,
facing page 249. The wager lost is a vignette within a page of text, and is
cropped to the picture alone.
